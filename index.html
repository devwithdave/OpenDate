How OpenDate Works: Technical Architecture

OpenDate is powered by the Nostr network (Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays), an open, decentralized protocol designed for sovereign human communication. Instead of relying on central corporate databases, predatory monthly paywalls, or data brokers, OpenDate connects your browser directly to independent public Nostr relays across the globe.

Learn more about the open Nostr protocol and its technical standards:
Visit nostr.com → Nostr Protocol Standards (NIPs) →
1. Sovereign Identity (NIP-01 & Password Managers)

When you create a profile, an elliptic curve keypair (secp256k1) is generated silently in browser memory. Hidden password manager hooks prompt iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager to back up your credentials with biometric protection (Face ID / Fingerprint). You own your account sovereignly with no phone number or email required.

2. Location Privacy (4-Character Geohash & 9-Cell Grid)

Exact street coordinates and high-precision GPS are never published. The location engine resolves your city or ZIP into a coarse 4-character geohash box (~25 mi x 12 mi). Discovery queries calculate the center cell plus its 8 perimeter neighbors (9 cells total), eliminating edge-boundary misses while preserving physical safety.

3. Decentralized Profiles (NIP-78 & NIP-40)

Profiles are published as parameterized replaceable application events (kind 30078). Every profile includes a 30-day auto-purge tag (NIP-40). Active daters renew this timestamp upon opening the app; inactive accounts are pruned from relay storage automatically.

4. Private Encrypted Likes & DMs (NIP-59 & NIP-17)

Likes and messages utilize 3-layer NIP-59 Gift Wrapping: Rumor (kind 14) is sealed (kind 13) with NIP-44 v2 encryption and wrapped (kind 1059) using a disposable burner key and randomized timestamp. Every message produces two wraps (one to partner, one to self-CC), synchronizing phones and desktops automatically from public relays.

5. Anti-Bot Defense (NIP-13 Proof of Work)

Outgoing likes and profile events run a lightweight 12-bit Proof-of-Work hash puzzle in non-blocking background loops. This takes ~1 second on mobile devices, keeping the platform smooth for humans while rendering mass commercial spam bots computationally uneconomical.

6. Safe Media (Blossom Protocol BUD-01)

All photos are loaded into an HTML5 canvas, scaled to 1080px, and re-encoded to JPEG (0.82), completely stripping all camera metadata and GPS coordinates before upload. Blobs are uploaded to Blossom media servers signed with kind 24242 Nostr authorization.

7. Muting & Trust (NIP-51 & NIP-56)

Blocked accounts are added to an encrypted personal mute list (kind 10000). Safety violations publish kind 1984 reports. Profiles with 3 or more community reports are hidden automatically.
